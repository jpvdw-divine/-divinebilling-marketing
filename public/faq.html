Straight answers

Why DivineBilling instead of Xero?

Xero Starter is R450/mo incl VAT for one organisation. Three entities are three licences (R1 350) before payroll and apps. Xero ZA is a superb ledger and practice tool. Divine Standard is R150/mo ex VAT for three companies, office seats included, unlimited contractor portal users, and the modules those people actually work in. Switch when you have outgrown a ledger.

Why DivineBilling instead of Sage?

Sage Accounting Start is from R240 incl VAT. Growth is sold as extras: user R75, company R410, inventory R415, debtors R290, time R165. Divine extra company or office seat is R119/mo. POS, HR, payroll and industry departments live in one product, not a Sage add-on catalogue. Pastel remains the heavier desktop cousin; we are cloud-native.

Why DivineBilling instead of QuickBooks?

QuickBooks ZA is a US product with a local landing page. Official billing is often USD; ZA resellers list Simple Start around R276. Simple Start is one user plus an accountant. There is no PSIRA roster, prepaid electricity, or free field portal. Divine bills in rand, hosts in South Africa, and does not charge a seat for every contractor.

Why DivineBilling instead of Wave?

Wave Starter is free for North American invoicing; Pro is US$19 per business; payroll is US/CA; card fees start at 2.9% + $0.60. It is not a SARS, POPIA or rand-first product. A Cape Town group on Wave is using someone else’s bookkeeping toy. Divine is built for that group’s companies, contractors and SARS calendar.

Is it actually cheaper after VAT?

Basic R100, Standard R150, Professional R250 per month ex VAT (live list, August 2026). After 15% VAT, Basic is about R115 versus Xero Starter R450. Three companies on Divine Standard ≈ R173 incl VAT versus Xero × 3 at R1 350, Sage Start + two extra companies at R1 060, or Wave Pro × 3 at about R1 055 (US$57). Setup is a one-time R500. Annual billing is 10 months for 12.

What do they still do better?

Xero and Sage: automated SA bank feeds and the accountant’s existing login. QuickBooks: a very simple books UI for one tiny entity. Wave: a $0 starter if you invoice in USD/CAD and never need SARS. Stay there if you run one trading entity, your practice lives in that stack, and you have no field staff. Come here when the operation is the product.

Who is DivineBilling for?

South African owners with contractors, multiple companies, or an industry department — security, utilities, retail/POS, butcher, farming — who want one login instead of Xero plus Excel plus a payroll bureau plus a roster app.
