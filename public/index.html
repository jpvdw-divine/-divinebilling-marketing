All questions →

Where is my data stored?

On servers in South Africa, with encrypted backups every day. Processing is aligned with POPIA, and SA ID and passport numbers stay masked until an authorised user reveals them.

Do contractors really cost nothing?

Yes. Contractors sign in to their own portal on every plan, with no limit. Only office seats and companies count toward your price.

Can I bring my existing data?

Import bank statements as CSV or PDF, and contractor timesheets from CSV or Excel. We walk owners and bookkeepers through setup.

What happens after the trial?

Pick a plan to keep going, or stop. No card is needed to start, and you can cancel anytime.
