FAQ

Is setup charged on Basic?

No. Setup is waived on Basic. Standard and Professional pay R250 once, ex VAT.

No. Contractor portal users are unlimited and free on every plan.

What does Standard cost after the introduction?

R299/month for your first 3 months, then R695/month, ex VAT.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel anytime. The free trial does not ask for a card.
