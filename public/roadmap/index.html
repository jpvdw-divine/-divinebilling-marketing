HR dashboard

Post-login home: profile, leave balance, pending requests, documents to sign, expiring PSIRA/qualifications, assigned site, announcements.

Live Phase 1

Employee file

Digital personnel file per employee — employee number, company number, SA ID number, contact details, emergency contact, next of kin, position, department, supervisor, employment date.

Live Phase 1

Document uploads

Employees upload ID, PSIRA, photo and qualifications from their phone. Secure storage per employee.

Live Phase 1

Qualification tracking

HR records qualification name, issue/expiry dates and certificate file. Automatic reminders at 90, 60, 30 days and when expired.

Live Phase 1

Site allocation

Every employee linked to a specific site → supervisor → client. Site name, address, shift pattern, contact numbers. Transfer history saved.

Live Phase 2

Leave management

Employees apply for leave from their phone. Workflow: employee → supervisor → HR → recorded. Balance, history and pending requests visible to employee and HR.

Live Phase 2

Electronic signing

Sign employment contract, disciplinary code and policies on phone — finger signature, timestamp, optional GPS. PDF stored permanently; HR gets confirmation.

Live Phase 2

Employee self-service

Mobile portal for employees: update phone/address, request banking change (HR approval), upload new qualifications, view & download signed contracts, submit leave.

Live Phase 3

HR admin portal

HR staff: add/edit employees, upload documents, assign sites, create contracts, approve leave, manage qualifications, track expiring docs, view full employee history.

Live Phase 3

Document management

Central searchable store: ID, PSIRA, CV, qualifications, contracts, warnings, performance reviews, medical certificates, leave forms — linked to employee file.
