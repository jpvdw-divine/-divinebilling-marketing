Multi-shop food retailers that replenish from one warehouse — not a generic WMS for 3PLs.

The problem

  • Nineteen shops WhatsApp the factory what they think they need.
  • Warehouse picks old stock first — or the wrong shop gets the last batch.
  • Inter-shop transfers hide that the warehouse still has product.

How it works

  1. Link each POS shop to the hub.
  2. Shops confirm and send restock; the hub verifies, signs off and submits.
  3. Warehouse picks FEFO, packs the route, delivers into shop stock.

What you get

Central control — inventory & ordering

One live view of linked shops: stock alerts, morning restock lists, then receive, verify, confirm, sign off and submit orders to production.

Morning control

Warehouse sitting, committed, in-transit, shop alerts, and what production dries tomorrow.

Warehouse

Finished goods, packaging and spices. Receive, QC, min/max, 7–10 day cover alerts.

Shop stock

Shop orders

Pick list

Dispatch

Yes — tills stay on POS. Central is the hub that receives those restock lists.

Can shops transfer stock to each other?

Not while the warehouse still has that product, unless you grant a same-day waiver.

Is this a tablet counting app?

Not in this cut — counts and write-offs are in the browser department.
