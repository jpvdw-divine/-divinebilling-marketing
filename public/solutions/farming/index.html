The problem

  • Feed is your biggest cost and you are guessing at the margin.
  • Egg counts, mortality and fertility live in a notebook.

How it works

  1. Register each flock and its buy-in cost.
  2. Log feed, daily eggs, fertility and health as you go.
  3. See profit per flock — feed in, eggs and sales out.

FAQ

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use WhatsApp on a phone, you can log eggs and feed.

Does it work with bad signal on the farm?

You need enough signal to save. We are not claiming a full offline cache yet.

Can I use it on my phone in the shed?

Yes — the farming screens are built for a phone.

Can I import my current records?

Yes, as part of onboarding — spreadsheets of flocks and opening costs.
