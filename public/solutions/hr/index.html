The problem

How it works

  1. Load the employee file (SA ID, sites, documents).

What you get

Email inbox capture

Connect IMAP — PDF invoices flagged for payment and assigned to income or expense categories.

HRMS — Personeel department

Full HR stack for site-based workforces (security PSIRA): employee file with SA ID, document uploads, qualification expiry, site allocation, mobile leave, e-sign from phone.

HR dashboard

Post-login home: profile, leave balance, pending requests, documents to sign, expiring PSIRA/qualifications, assigned site, announcements.

Employee file

Digital personnel file per employee — employee number, company number, SA ID number, contact details, emergency contact, next of kin, position, department, supervisor, employment date.

Document uploads

Employees upload ID, PSIRA, photo and qualifications from their phone. Secure storage per employee.

Qualification tracking

HR records qualification name, issue/expiry dates and certificate file. Automatic reminders at 90, 60, 30 days and when expired.

Site allocation

Every employee linked to a specific site → supervisor → client. Site name, address, shift pattern, contact numbers. Transfer history saved.

Leave management

Employees apply for leave from their phone. Workflow: employee → supervisor → HR → recorded. Balance, history and pending requests visible to employee and HR.
