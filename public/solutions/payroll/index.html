FAQ

Do you eFile EMP201?

We calculate monthly PAYE, UIF and SDL totals for EMP201. You submit to SARS. Direct eFiling is not claimed here.

Are contractors on the pay run?

Contractors bill through the portal. Employees sit on the pay run. Both land in Finance.
