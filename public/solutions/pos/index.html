Built for SA retailers and butcher counters — catch-weight kg, VAT receipts and takings that post to Finance in the same workspace.

The problem

  • Cashing up by hand every night; the till never matches the books.
  • Stock counts that are always out of date.
  • No idea which store or which product actually makes money.
  • Staff hours tracked on paper.

How it works

  1. Load your catalogue and stores.
  2. Staff sell and clock in on the till.
  3. Sales, stock and VAT flow into Finance — cash-up is done.

What you get

POS — multi-store till

Shop terminals on the same cooler as Butcher — catalogue, catch-weight kg, 15% VAT receipts, transfers, time clock; staff from HR, takings posted to Finance.

Dashboard & alerts

Today’s takings, low stock, staff on the clock and shop-by-shop visibility.

POS terminal

Scan or pick products, catch-weight kg, 15% VAT and FEFO deduction on the Butcher cooler.

Stores

Shop locations. Admins see all stores; managers stay on their own shop.

Products

FAQ

Does it work offline during load-shedding?

The till needs a connection to post sales today. A proper offline mode is on the roadmap — we will not claim it until it ships.

Which card machines does it support?

None as a native integration yet. Use your existing card device and record the tender on the till.

Do I need special hardware?

A browser and a tablet or PC is enough to start. Confirm scanners and printers with us first.

Can I run more than one store?

Yes — multi-store is the point of the module.

How do I move off my current till?

We import catalogue and opening stock as part of onboarding.
