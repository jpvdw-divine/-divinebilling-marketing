FAQ

Is it PSIRA compliant?

We store PSIRA grade on the employee file and keep the operational record (occurrence book, incidents, posts) in one place. Full statutory compliance remains your responsibility as the registered employer.

Can guards log incidents on their phones?

Yes — the occurrence book and incident forms are built for a phone in the field.

Does it handle payroll and EMP201?

Yes, when you switch on Payroll. Hours and contractor billing sit next to the pay run.

Can I bill each client separately per site?

Yes — sites map to client accounts so you invoice per site from the same workspace.

What happens to the data if I leave?

You can export your records. Daily encrypted backups are kept while the account is active.
