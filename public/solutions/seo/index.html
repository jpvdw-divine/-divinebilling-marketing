The problem

  • Findings, briefs, and approvals live in different documents.
  • A draft can change after someone thought they had signed it off.
  • Client Reports already covers site health. Seo work still needs its own record.

How it works

  1. Add the website, its client, and the facts you are willing to cite.
  2. Record findings and an internal priority, then write the draft against those facts.
  3. A different person approves the exact hash, target, and expiry.

FAQ

Does this publish to the website?

No. Approved means the change is ready for a later publishing phase. There is no publish, schedule, or rollback action here.

Does it crawl the site or pull Google data?

No. Staff record findings, facts, and drafts. Nothing is crawled or fetched from Google in this workspace.

Is the opportunity number a Google score?

No. It is an internal priority. Missing parts of the score are left out. They are not stored as zero.

Can the person who wrote the draft approve it?

No. The approver has to be a different user, and the approval is tied to that draft’s content hash.
