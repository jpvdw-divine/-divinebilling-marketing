FAQ

Can we buy only this?

Yes. The Voting OS plan can be the only department on the workspace. Finance and the other departments stay off.

Can it wear our colours?

Yes. Save a party name and colours, or switch on white-label so the sidebar and footer use the party name.

Does it message voters by itself?

No. It records who may be contacted. A banned WhatsApp account is not a shortcut this product will take.
