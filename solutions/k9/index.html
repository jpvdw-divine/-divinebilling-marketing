Security companies that deploy dogs with guards — not a pet-clinic app.

The problem

  • Dog files in a notebook; handlers in HR; invoices in accounting.
  • No view of which dogs are deployment-ready.
  • Site billing forgets the K9 unit on the post.

How it works

  1. Register each dog and pair a handler from HR.
  2. Log training, medical and site deployments.
  3. Invoice the linked client for dogs and guards on that site.
